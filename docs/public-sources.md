# Public-source audit — 7 October 2026

The product needs many **usable incident records**, not a large number of statistical rows. Publication counts, crime totals and court decisions are different units. No completeness or crime-probability claim is made.

See the [fresh regional sources and implementation plan](fresh-source-plan.md) for the expanded oblast inventory, verified public channels, event-date examples and changes needed before increasing collection frequency.

`apps/api/src/ingestion/sources.ts` is the source registry, with 16 active sources and one archived court source. A configured source is not necessarily available or producing map records. `/api/v1/status` and the Settings sheet show successful checks, outages/retries, archive status and published counts. The four media additions above were checked on 7 October; this records access and adapter configuration, not a claim that they have already increased the production counter.

The four new media profiles scan at most 160 RSS entries, retain at most 12 candidate originals per poll and use a seven-day publication lookback. They check publisher robots policies, exact article hosts/paths and full-text availability, omit recommendation blocks and stop on changed layouts or explicit access blocks. No DOM is built for the entire multi-megabyte feed. Dnepr and 061 feed texts are used directly; Poltava and LB candidate articles are fetched separately. Collection is at most hourly. The publication lookback does not establish an event date: an article published today can concern an older incident, a trial or an update to an existing report.

## Archived court history

The September court archive examined locally contains 47,544 active criminal verdicts across 2026, including 5,838 verdicts published since September 1. Across the last 90 publication days, 6,193 verdicts matched the initial category whitelist. These are **candidates**, not automatically geocoded incidents. In a 24-document theft/robbery/fraud sample, three yielded explicit event dates and named settlements; 21 stayed unpublished. Many judgments redact event addresses or concern several episodes.

Historical court location extraction starts after `ВСТАНОВИВ` and requires a dated event paragraph with an event verb and an explicitly named settlement. The court heading, residence and birthplace are excluded. Martial-law decree dates are not event dates. Conflicting places/dates and unconfirmed categories remain private for review. Court canonical identity is `court-document:<externalId>`: a shared case number does not prove that separate judgments describe the same episode. The previous importer supported registry-confirmed withdrawals. With collection disabled, the authenticated court upload, manual processing and queue consumer refuse new court work; existing public cards and their source links remain. A court marker describes an event discussed in a judgment; it does not imply a new crime on the judgment's publication date.

## Regional Telegram contract and first pilot

The common collector uses each source's configured channel, GeoNames region code, exact canonical-host allowlist and verification URL. Source coverage is metadata, not evidence of an incident's location. Polling runs hourly with a minimum per-source interval of 60 minutes; retry backoff and resource limits can delay an individual check. Only the five independently confirmed regional channels above are activated. Verification links and how they were read are recorded in the [source plan](fresh-source-plan.md). Access to government pages varies by host and request route, separately from successful live Telegram requests.

Messages retain their channel-specific IDs and publication timestamps. Collection checks robots rules for the selected channel and pagination path, rejects redirects and unexpected layouts, bounds page/response sizes, and advances numeric cursors through media-only posts. Canonical article links must use HTTPS, an exact configured host and a permitted news path, without credentials or a nonstandard port; they are recorded as provenance and never followed by the Telegram collector. Cross-channel messages cannot supply another source's items or cursor. An actual Telegram forwarding header sets `isRepost`; a canonical article link alone does not. No original publication time is invented.

Event date, source publication date and retrieval date remain separate. Relative event dates use the verified original publication time and `Europe/Kyiv`; a late repost without a proven original time cannot anchor “yesterday.” Report, detention, investigation and judgment dates do not become event dates unless the text explicitly ties them to the episode. Records with unknown event dates can appear in publication-date mode, but are excluded from event-date filtering.

On 6 October 2026 the first production pilot fetched **three pages per channel** and drained all five regional queues. It added **5 public records**: Rivne 2, Chernivtsi 1 and Zhytomyr 2; Vinnytsia and Volyn added 0, with geography review the main publication bottleneck. Among the five new records, **1** has an event date within the last seven days (Korosten, 5 October), **1** describes an older event (26 August), and **3** have unknown event dates. The regional pending queue was 0 at that check. Public count rose from **101 to 106 before legacy-record reprocessing**; this is a pilot snapshot, not the final count after subsequent reprocessing. Three pages do not establish a complete 7–14-day history or oblast coverage.

## Other sources and existing projects checked

- [CrimeDataLab](https://crimedatalab.org/) and [its author's public repositories](https://github.com/Nickolay78) aggregate prosecution and judicial statistics. Millions of **statistical indicators** are not millions of geolocated incidents. Useful for a future statistics view; not used to manufacture map points.
- [ZAXID's Lviv crime map](https://zaxid.net/projects/crime/) and [project description](https://zaxid.net/karta_zlochinnosti_lvova_n1455334) demonstrate official address-based police data for 2015–2017. This is historical coverage, not a current national feed. No unverified export is imported.
- [Illegal weapons open dataset](https://data.gov.ua/dataset/1f6730f3-b326-4466-b724-3b88cf2aceda) and [murder/severe injury dataset](https://data.gov.ua/dataset/bcc3410c-a585-4576-86fd-93b674c6a456) are aggregate counts/rates. They need a separate statistical unit, period and territorial definition.
- [Court search](https://reyestr.court.gov.ua/) was unreliable from the test environment. The separate official `od.reyestr.court.gov.ua/files/...rtf` open-data host worked for historical imports; archived public citations link to the original Review page.
- [National Police open data](https://npu.gov.ua/diyalnist/vidkriti-dani) and [MVS search](https://mvs.gov.ua/activity/rozsuk) remain candidates for separately defined datasets. Wanted-person records are not incident locations.
- Suspilne direct homepage access returned 403; no functioning feed was verified in this audit, so no working connector is claimed.
- [Bellingcat Ukraine Timemap](https://github.com/bellingcat/ukraine-timemap) concerns civilian harm during war. It is not mixed into ordinary crime categories.

## Attribution and reuse

Public cards contain constrained bilingual factual summaries and direct publisher links, not republished articles. Original source text is private with 90-day retention. Access is restricted to allowlisted hosts/paths, checks robots policies and stops on explicit blocks. Robots access alone does not confer a content licence. ZAXID's disallowed archive route is not used; its public monthly sitemaps are used instead.

Settlement coordinates and current Ukrainian names derive from [GeoNames Ukraine data](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/) and [language-tagged alternate names](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/alternatenames/). GeoNames is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The generated gazetteer contains 3,266 populated places with recorded population at least 1,000 nationally and 5,791 smaller settlements in Vinnytsia, Rivne, Volyn, Chernivtsi, Zhytomyr and Lviv oblasts, excluding neighbourhood features. This totals 9,057 places; it is not every settlement in Ukraine. Smaller-settlement matches require explicit or source regional context. Same-oblast homonyms remain unpublished without further geographic proof (for example, the two Rokytne places in Rivne oblast). Dates such as «30 вересня» and regional references such as «на Буковині» do not become matches to similarly named villages. Coordinates are settlement centres, not reported street addresses. `scripts/build-gazetteer.py` reproduces both JSON files from the official country files. Attribution appears in Settings as well as here.

## Delivery and remaining quality work

Working RSS/article adapters, protected bounded media imports, durable queues, source retry state, event-date-aware filtering and provenance labels are implemented. Court harvesting is disabled in the Worker, the local Python entrypoint and the local GitHub workflow. The disabled workflow is manual-only and its job cannot run; its remote schedule disappears when the workflow change is pushed. After the Worker release, even an older scheduled workflow receives authenticated HTTP 410 without reading or writing D1. GitHub was the runner for the official registry, not the data source. No historical court cards are deleted as part of this change.

The Free plan budget keeps the existing global ceiling of 640 process-only jobs per UTC day. With 16 hourly poll jobs, normal Queue usage is at most 3,072 read/write/delete operations per day before retries and recovery, leaving headroom below the 10,000-operation allowance. Web-source retries back off and never increase the configured polling frequency.

Next quality work is a review/correction interface, more independently verified regional sources, more complete settlement morphology and measured cross-publisher duplicate resolution. Headline similarity is only a review signal. Current summaries preserve a limited set of safe facts; they are not complete translations. A growing map is still a map of public reports with partial geographic and editorial coverage.
